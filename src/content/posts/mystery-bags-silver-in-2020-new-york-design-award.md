Just got an exciting email that the [Mystery Bag project I led at Trident](https://andrewhy.de/business-unusual-selling-used-books-in-the-time-of-covid-19/) (I’m no longer an owner as of June!) just won a Silver in the Service – Community category for the 2020 New York Design Awards!

2020 has been a year of super highs and super lows… and I’m just going to take this as a super nice high. 🙂

What a fun time to rise up and try to save a local icon of a shop.
